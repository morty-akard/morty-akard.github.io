Market Research Platform

BetaTrends is a market research platform that helps businesses and creators validate ideas, understand their audience, and refine their products through data-driven insights. By leveraging interactive user engagement, BetaTrends enables companies to test concepts, gather real-time feedback, and optimize their offerings before launch.
Designed for both businesses seeking market validation and influencers looking to monetise their reach, BetaTrends bridges the gap between creators and audiences, ensuring every decision is backed by actual user preferences.

Cloud Gaming Platform

Arium is a cloud gaming platform that empowers casual gamers by unlocking earning potential from playing their favorite games. As a designer on the team, I helped design most features within three months using various design methods, including user experience design, design system, and A/B testing. I played a significant role in ensuring the product's success by creating half of the product designs, maintaining the design system, and helping my teammates learn new tools. Overall, this experience allowed me to hone my skills in designing digital products and contribute to the creation of an innovative gaming platform.

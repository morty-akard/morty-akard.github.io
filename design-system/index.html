Colours have specific roles

Warm cream is reserved for headlines and titles; neutral light grey supports primary reading text. Warm orange marks primary links and selection. The stronger action orange and teal evidence colour retain their existing uses. Purple belongs to project treatments; colours within screenshots are independent of this palette.

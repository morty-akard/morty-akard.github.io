## Web implementation guidance

- Keep the product screen—not a decorative metaphor—as the hero.
- Keep the hero loop user-controlled and muted; never autoplay audio.
- Lazy-load below-the-fold videos and retain direct links as fallbacks.
- Stack inherited-flow and finding-response modules on narrow screens.
- Give journey artifacts an open/zoom affordance in the final website.
- Preserve captions and evidence qualifications beside their visuals.
- Confirm publication rights and redact identifying data before public release.

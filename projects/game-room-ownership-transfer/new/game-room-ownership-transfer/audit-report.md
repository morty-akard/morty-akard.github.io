### Executive diagnosis

The case study has a genuine turning point: Morty's initial right-click assumption produced no direct successes in a 24-participant Maze task, so he exposed the menu through a visible trigger. The strongest evidence is the link between observed paths and a specific interaction revision. The skill-led improvement starts with the revised direction, uses insight-led headings, removes two low-value visuals from the reading path, and makes the evidence boundary explicit. The remaining presentation issue is the legibility of small text inside some raw screenshots; the remaining evidence limitation is the absence of a preserved retest.

## Story and scan-path review

- **Previous structure:** Opening explanation → project metadata → generic Challenge → initial interaction → testing → revision → learning.
- **Revised structure:** Final direction and evidence boundary preview → orientation → hidden starting point → failed right-click assumption → testing turning point → visible-trigger response → open validation and learning.
- **Opening relevance:** Yes. It shows the affected user, initial weakness, response and limitation before asking the reader to continue.
- **Headings alone:** They communicate the problem, assumption, test finding, response and remaining validation.
- **Activities connected to decisions:** Yes. The HMW framing establishes the task; low-fidelity work enables early testing; test paths identify the entry problem; the visible trigger responds to it.
- **Transformation mapped to struggle:** Yes, at the level supported by evidence. The revision exposes the hidden entry point; improved completion is not claimed.

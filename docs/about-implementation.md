# About reading layout

The original paragraph wording and order are retained. Two h3 subheadings
within the opening story identify the transition into product design and
the present-day approach. The story uses the existing 760px reading token;
other About sections retain their existing layouts.

Page-specific styles live in `css/about.css` under `.about-page`. Typography,
spacing, reading width, and text colours reuse foundation tokens. Primary
prose uses `--color-copy`; the new subheadings and other section headings use
`--color-heading`. The opening question retains its existing orange accent
as an intentional part of the page's identity. The car illustration keeps
its natural proportions and existing responsive float/stack behaviour.

The turning-point sentence uses the existing medium weight, and the closing
question has extra space before its lead-in. No text is duplicated or hidden.
